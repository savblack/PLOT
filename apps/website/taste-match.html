Taste match

How alike is your taste?

Plot looks at the films and shows you and a friend have both rated and turns them into one number. Then it shows the genres you share, what you both loved and the ones you’ll argue about.

Find your match New to Plot? Sign up

What you’ll see

Your match
A score from 0 to 100 for how closely your star ratings line up on the titles you’ve both rated.
Genres side by side
How much of each of your histories is drama, comedy, horror and the rest.
You both loved
The titles you both gave four stars or more.
Where you disagree
The titles you rated two stars or more apart, widest gap first.
Decades and critics
The decade each of you watches most, and which of you hands out fewer stars.
Share the result
Turn your match into a card for Instagram, Threads or X.

Only what they already share

A comparison uses the watch history and ratings the other person already shows you: anyone with a public profile, or a private account that has accepted your follow. Shared cards say “a friend” unless their profile is public and you choose to add their name.

Taste match is part of the deeper stats in Plot Premium. The person you compare with doesn’t need Premium.
