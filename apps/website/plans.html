Questions

Is the Free plan really free?
Yes. No credit card and no trial clock. Track as much as you like on Free, for as long as you like.
Can I get Premium now?
Not yet. Premium is coming soon at US$3/month or US$24/year, taxes included. Upgrading in the app shows a coming-soon message and doesn’t take a payment.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Once Premium opens, cancel from Manage subscription in Settings and keep Premium until the end of the period you’ve paid for.
What happens to my lists if I downgrade?
Nothing is deleted. Your lists stay exactly as they are. You just can’t create new ones past the free limit until you upgrade again.

Prices are in US dollars and include tax. Your total and billing currency are shown at checkout. Cancel anytime. Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Need a hand? contact@theplot.tv
