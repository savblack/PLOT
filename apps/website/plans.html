Questions

Is the Free plan really free?
Yes — no credit card, no trial clock. Track as much as you want on Free for as long as you like.
Can I cancel anytime?
Anytime, in one click from Settings. You keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for.
What happens to my lists if I downgrade?
Nothing is deleted. Your lists stay exactly as they are — you just can’t create new ones past the free limit until you upgrade again.
How does sync work?
Connect Plex or Trakt once and PLOT keeps your watchlist and history current automatically — including what you watch on Netflix, Prime, Disney+ and more.
