Loader Concepts

Route transition loader options — pick one to implement in PlotLoader.jsx

Current — Spinning arc
Short arc rotates around the O. Clean and fast-feeling.
Accent arc
Same spin but the arc uses the brand pink. More expressive.
Dual spin
Outer white arc + inner pink arc spin opposite directions. Dynamic and bold.
Shimmer glint
A soft highlight sweeps left to right across the full wordmark. Very polished.
Letter stagger
P → L → O → T each flare bright in sequence on repeat. Expressive and rhythmic.
Orbit dot
A pink dot travels the circumference of the O. Playful, planet-like motion.
Breathe
The whole wordmark gently pulses opacity. Ultra minimal — lets the brand breathe.
Dots below
Static wordmark + three bouncing dots. Familiar, readable loading metaphor.
