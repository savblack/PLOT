Foot bar · what changed and why
  1. One bar, one shape. Today the foot swaps between "Watched it?", "How was it?" and the collapsed take bar, each a different layout, so it jumps as you act. Now it is always the same grid: a head line (Watched it? or Thoughts?, with the watch status chip on the right), then stars on the left and the one next action on the right, then the written review as a quote card once there is one.
  2. Stars always work. They are live in the collapsed bar, so rating is one tap, half stars included. Tapping a star on an unwatched title logs it as watched and rates it in the same gesture.
  3. No dead end. "Mark this watched to rate it" is gone. Review and private note boxes only appear once the title is watched, and each has a plus when empty and a pencil when written, as the design system describes.
  4. No nag, so no "Not yet" and no snooze. The bar is quiet and always there, so there is nothing to dismiss.
  5. Marking watched is forgiving. The bar confirms in place with Undo and a date change, then settles on its own after six seconds. It never opens a sheet unasked.
  6. Reviews write in place. On the panel the editor grows upward from the bar. On a phone it is a sheet. Rating saves immediately, and an unsaved draft survives closing.
  7. One source of truth. The Watched pill in the action row and the bar always agree. I've watched appears in the bar only while the title is unwatched.
