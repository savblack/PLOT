# Keyboard Accessibility Checklist

Issue: `SUS-17`

- Search results: Tab to a result row, press `Enter` and `Space`, confirm the media panel opens and the save, favourite, and watched buttons still work independently.
- Discover: Tab to the hero card, trending cards, weekly chart rows, and platform cards, then activate each with `Enter` and `Space`.
- History: Tab to a history row and confirm keyboard activation opens the selected title.
- My Lists: Tab to watching rows, want-to-watch rows, top 10 populated rows, favourites posters, and custom-list posters; confirm each opens details without triggering nested remove buttons.
- Settings: Tab to sign out, streaming platforms, channels, region, timezone, import, feedback, clear history, clear watchlist, and delete account; confirm each opens the expected flow and disabled destructive rows are skipped while busy.
- Onboarding: Tab to provider cards and seed-title cards, press `Enter` and `Space`, and confirm the selected state toggles visually and semantically.
