Three more takes on combining stats with diary entries

Version 4 — Timeline + Milestones

Version 5 — Split Pane

Fixed stats panel on the left, scrollable diary on the right. Both always visible — like a dashboard with a live record. You can read deep into the diary while the stats panel anchors your year-level picture. The left panel doesn't change as you scroll right.

Version 6 — Poster-Forward

Each month is a visual card led by a strip of poster art. Key month stats sit below the posters before the entry list. Older months collapse into a single row showing just posters + summary stats — expand to see entries. More visual, more cinematic. The current month is always fully expanded.
