Version 3

No separate stats section — the data lives inside the diary. Each month opens with a row of mini-stat cards (that month's count, avg rating, top mood, rating sparkline). Genre pills show what you were gravitating to. The stats tell the story of each month, not just the year. The AI read sits at the top as an always-visible banner.
