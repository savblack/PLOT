## In theaters

*Resident Evil* sends a medical courier into a night that keeps getting worse. [The first-review roundup](https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/article/resident-evil-2026-first-reviews/) finds critics enjoying its speed, scares, and sense of play. Zach Cregger's horror instincts look like a good match for this particular delivery job.

*Practical Magic 2* brings Sandra Bullock and Nicole Kidman back to the family business. [Variety finds the reunion overlong and predictable](https://au.variety.com/2026/film/global/practical-magic-2-review-sandra-bullock-nicole-kidman-40077/). The sisters remain the invitation; adjust your expectations for the spell itself.

*The Weight* gives Ethan Hawke a grim trek through the wilderness with stolen gold and very little reason to trust his traveling companions. [Film Obsessive likes the physical suspense more than the finish](https://filmobsessive.com/film/new-releases/the-weight-pushes-desperation-until-the-end/). A dirt-under-the-fingernails alternative to another franchise night.

## What everyone's talking about

*Resident Evil* seems to have found the pleasure in being underqualified for a horror movie. In the [opening discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/movies/comments/1wjd97x/official_disucssion_resident_evil_2026_spoilers/), early viewers respond to a lead who feels vulnerable rather than invincible, and to the humor threaded through the panic. That matters more than recognizing every reference. You can understand being terrified and out of options without having held a controller. [Keep Resident Evil on your radar](https://theplot.tv/movie/resident-evil-1423191).

*Monster: The Lizzie Borden Story* has a harder problem: what does another dramatization add? [RogerEbert.com finds arresting performances and imagery inside a frustrating, overstuffed series](https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/monster-the-lizzie-borden-story-ryan-murphy-netflix-tv-review-2026). The [Guardian also faults its handling of the subject](https://www.theguardian.com/tv-and-radio/2026/sep/17/monster-the-lizzie-borden-story-review-netflix). The objection is about the telling, rather than a shortage of lurid material. If true crime already leaves you wondering who benefits from the retelling, this one brings that question along with it.

## Now on TV and Streaming

![Best of the Best promotional backdrop](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w780/5m1j7Wibh3HnZq5QHm97v8dI3GH.jpg)

Image: Best of the Best (2026), promotional backdrop. Source: [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/movie/1514863).

*Best of the Best* puts two childhood friends on UCLA's competitive Bollywood-fusion dance team. [It arrived on Netflix September 18](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/best-of-the-best-plot-cast-release-date-news). The draw is friendship under pressure, big routines, and the very college-age belief that a competition can determine your entire future. For the part of you that misses a proper dance movie.

![The Fixers promotional backdrop](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w780/iyHgIyMJ6Vd5w79dLeGOTOkqDS5.jpg)

Image: The Fixers (2026), promotional backdrop. Source: [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/tv/312663).

*The Fixers* pairs a former gangster with a politician's son to clean up other people's dirty secrets. [The new series landed on Netflix September 17](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/what-to-watch-on-netflix-september-11-2026). Pick this when you want a crime setup where the people solving the problem may be another problem.
